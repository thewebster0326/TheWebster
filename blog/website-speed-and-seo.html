How Website Speed Affects Your SEO Ranking

Google has confirmed that page experience — including how quickly a page loads — is part of its ranking algorithm. A slow site doesn't just frustrate visitors; it can actively hold your rankings back, especially now that Google indexes and ranks primarily based on the mobile version of your site, where connections are often slower and less reliable than desktop.

Why speed matters beyond rankings

Speed affects more than search visibility. Visitors decide almost instantly whether to stay on a page or leave, and a slow load is one of the fastest ways to lose someone before they've even seen what you offer. A faster site keeps more of the visitors your SEO work already earned.

What actually slows a site down

  • Unoptimized images — full-resolution photos loaded at thumbnail size
  • Render-blocking scripts and stylesheets that delay the page from displaying
  • Bloated page-builder plugins that load far more code than a page needs
  • Poor hosting that struggles under real traffic

What we do about it

Every site we build is checked against this from day one — compressed images, minimal render-blocking resources, and clean, purpose-built code instead of a page-builder's default bloat. Speed isn't an afterthought added at the end of a project; it's a constraint we design within from the start.
